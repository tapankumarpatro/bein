# Far atoms for design

Each line is a domain and an atom. Rewrite the atom as one self-contained sentence for the slot when you use it.

## form

- **restaurants:** *Omakase.* The maker decides and the user only sets limits, which removes choice overload.
- **restaurants:** *Mise en place.* Everything needed is laid out before the work starts, with nothing fetched mid-task.
- **architecture:** *Threshold space.* A deliberate in-between zone marks the change from one mode to another.
- **architecture:** *Desire paths.* Let usage wear the route, then pave the worn paths.
- **editorial:** *Contact sheet.* Every option is shown small and at once, so you choose by comparing, not by browsing.
- **editorial:** *Marginalia.* Commentary lives beside the content, not on top of it.
- **editorial:** *Index.* A dense, alphabetical, jump-anywhere map of everything inside.
- **film:** *Storyboard.* A sequence of key frames shows the whole arc before any detail.
- **film:** *Establishing shot.* Show the wide context once, then cut close.
- **retail:** *Receipt.* A short, final, itemised proof of what just happened.
- **theme parks:** *Queue theatre.* Waiting time is filled with content that prepares you for what comes next.
- **libraries:** *Card catalogue.* Small physical-feeling records, sortable in several orders.
- **cartography:** *Map with legend.* Space shows the relationships, and a key decodes the symbols.
- **music:** *Score.* Parallel tracks along one shared timeline.
- **games:** *Diegetic UI.* Information lives inside the world (a dashboard on the car), not in an overlay.
- **social ritual:** *Guestbook.* Each visitor leaves a lasting mark for the next one.

## medium

- **time:** The interface changes with the hour, the season or the user's own rhythm.
- **sound:** Short earcons carry state, so you need not look.
- **haptics:** Taps or detents confirm actions without sight.
- **print:** A weekly physical or PDF artefact summarises the digital work.
- **email:** A digest arrives at a chosen moment, so there is no app to open.
- **ambient light:** Peripheral colour signals status at a glance.
- **voice:** A conversational turn replaces a form.
- **paper prototype:** The design is made deliberately rough, to invite edits.
- **the user's own files:** The tool lives inside the folder or document they already use.

## interaction

- **manufacturing:** *Poka-yoke.* The wrong action is made impossible by shape, not by warnings.
- **games:** *Juice.* Every action gets an immediate, proportionate, delightful response.
- **games:** *Unlockables.* Advanced controls appear only once earned through use.
- **games:** *Save slots.* Keep parallel versions to branch and return to.
- **video editing:** *Scrubbing.* Drag through time to preview any state.
- **physical products:** *Detents.* Snap to meaningful values as you drag.
- **banking:** *Two-key commit.* A big action needs a deliberate second gesture.
- **theatre:** *Rehearsal.* Run the whole thing in a safe dry-run mode before the real one.
- **conversation:** *Call and response.* The system proposes, the user answers, and the turns are short.
- **research:** *Wizard of Oz.* A human fakes the hard part while the experience is tested.
- **rituals:** *Ceremony.* A small repeated act marks starting or finishing.
- **cartography:** *Pan and zoom.* Semantic zoom shows different detail at different scales.
- **collaboration:** *Live cursors.* Others' presence is visible where they are working.
